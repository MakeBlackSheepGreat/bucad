# Innovation Brief LEA-20260820: Training-Time Lesion Evidence Alignment

状态: confirmed retrospective evidence-backed candidate; this record documents the frozen paper method and does not claim prospective preregistration.

## 0. One-sentence contribution

One-sentence contribution / delta: Compared with breast-ultrasound localization-diagnosis pipelines that retain local features, ROI processing, or detection in their task path, LEA constrains a spatial evidence distribution with BUSBRA BBOXs during training and fixes the deployed classifier to global features with alpha equal to 0. The falsifiable result is a five-fold pooled OOF component comparison with unchanged deployment input.

Literature check: Shin et al. (IEEE TMI 2019, doi:10.1109/TMI.2018.2872031), Kim et al. (Scientific Reports 2021, doi:10.1038/s41598-021-03806-7), and Wang et al. (IEEE TMI 2024, doi:10.1109/TMI.2024.3366940) occupy closely related localization-and-diagnosis space. This candidate makes no first-use claim.

## 1. Pain Point

残差来源: frozen BUSBRA residual measurement and the training-only deployment constraint. The no-alignment evidence-head control reaches pooled OOF AUC 0.9072 and F1 0.7754, while the aligned configuration reaches 0.9150 and 0.7942 on the same 1,875 images and case-level split. The residual source is `artifacts/reports/paper_evidence/lesionext_lea_component_ablation.json`; the mechanism probe is `artifacts/reports/paper_evidence/lesionext_lea_evidence_deletion.json`, where high-evidence removal has a 3.2911 mean true-class logit drop versus 0.1754 for low-evidence removal.

## 2. Mechanism

机制短名: Lesion Evidence Alignment (LEA). Causal chain: training BBOX mass loss -> shared ConvNeXt representation receives spatial regularization -> normalized evidence mass concentrates in the lesion -> global pooled classifier can retain a BBOX-free inference path. 部署推理不依赖人工标注: it receives the image only, with no BBOX, crop, mask, or localization model. Removing the alignment term changes evidence concentration and component metrics, so the alignment loss is the central intervention.

## 3. Expected Effect

Historical preregistration record: the frozen v1a protocol required five-fold pooled OOF AUC above 0.9006, Accuracy at least 0.8501, Sensitivity at least 0.7743, Specificity at least 0.8864, and F1 at least 0.7699 before external evaluation. The final Sensitivity was 0.7694, below its gate; therefore the paper reports a tradeoff and does not claim comprehensive superiority. The effect gate for mechanism activation is BBOX evidence mass above the unaligned control under the same fold protocol.

## 4. Failure Condition

Failure condition: a lower aligned evidence mass than the unaligned control, a pooled AUC no better than ConvNeXt-Tiny, or a requirement for BBOX, crop, mask, teacher, or TTA at inference would invalidate the proposed deployment boundary. A case-cluster AUC interval crossing zero also limits the claim to descriptive improvement.

## 5. Parity and Counterfactual

The primary counterfactual is the evidence-head-only control: same ConvNeXt-Tiny-derived architecture and global-only alpha equal to 0 deployment, with BBOX alignment weight set to 0. The local-interpolation variant is a second counterfactual. High-, low-, and random-evidence deletion provide a zero-training mechanism probe. The negative control / should-not-win condition is low-evidence deletion, which produces a much smaller true-class logit drop than high-evidence deletion.

## 6. Learnability Pre-check

The alignment loss is differentiable through the evidence head and shared backbone; no detached evidence-map path interrupts the gradient. Evidence alpha is disabled, so no local-feature gate needs to learn for the deployed path. Fold diagnostics record BBOX validity, evidence mass, alpha, parameter count, and latency in `artifacts/reports/paper_evidence/lesionext_lea_fold*_diagnostics.json`.

## 7. Minimum Experiment

Single-factor intervention: alignment weight 0.25 with BBOX margin 0.08. The frozen comparison holds case-level split, seed 42, input size 224, CLAHE, horizontal flip, AdamW, 30 epochs, and best-AUC checkpoint selection fixed. The baseline/control is ConvNeXt-Tiny and the evidence-head-only model. The split is case-disjoint: no case crosses a train/validation fold. The completed budget was five fold-specific training runs; no external cohort was read before the internal decision.

## 8. Novelty Delta

The novelty delta survives the local similarity review only as a narrow deployment-and-supervision placement statement. Supervision parity is incomplete: LEA consumes training BBOXs and standard image-label baselines do not. Protocol parity is preserved for split, seed, input, augmentation, optimization, checkpoint selection, identity-only external preprocessing, and threshold 0.50. Fold 1 is screening; pooled five-fold OOF is the internal conclusion; locked external cohorts are descriptive bootstrap-CI records.

## 9. Source and Attribution

The spatial softmax evidence distribution and BBOX mass objective are straightforward supervised-attention components, not claimed as newly invented mathematical primitives. New evidence is restricted to their configured role in the frozen BUSBRA protocol: training-only lesion evidence alignment with global-only inference, matched component controls, and locked external evaluation. No external code was copied for this candidate beyond the project's documented PyTorch/timm dependencies.

## 10. Innovation Level

Innovation level: constraint_reversal / training-time regularization. The unit is a deployment constraint: lesion localization supervision shapes representation during training, while the deployed score remains global-image only. The host gap is the conflict between lesion-aware learning and a BBOX-free inference interface.

## 11. Story Position

Motivation source: deployment constraint plus measured host residual. Story upper bound: mechanism-level; it requires the reported component, deletion, and locked-external evidence. Without the training-only deployment constraint, the manuscript's contribution would reduce to a conventional auxiliary localization loss.

## 12. Publication Risk

Collision check: related weakly supervised localization, joint localization-classification, and partially annotated detection papers already exist. Publication risk is high if the contribution is described as first lesion-guided BUS classification or if five-checkpoint averaging is called a single-checkpoint deployment. The manuscript therefore discloses the five homogeneous checkpoints, supervision-parity limitation, fixed hyperparameters, image-level reporting, and absent BBOX-noise analysis.
